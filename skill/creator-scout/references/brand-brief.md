# Brand brief (summary of `scout/brand.yaml`)

The client buys, refurbishes and sells gaming PCs with a warranty across 20+
European markets, priced about 20% below the cheapest non-peer-to-peer
alternative. Buyers' main hesitation is trust in refurbished hardware, so the
most valuable content shows the product honestly: builds, benchmarks, setup
tours, upgrade stories, "is refurbished worth it" videos.

A good partner is a real creator whose audience plays PC or console games or
cares about PC hardware. Gameplay, streaming and gaming-culture creators all
qualify; creators who already make trust content rank higher. Small creators
with engaged audiences are welcome — they are the point.

Competitors and competing retailers are listed in `scout/brand.yaml`.
Sponsorship by one of them is flagged as a risk, not a reason to reject.

Typical deals: gifting (a loaner or discounted PC) plus an affiliate code for
creators under ~10k followers; paid integration plus affiliate above that; an
"upgrade for content" deal when a creator shows an old setup.
