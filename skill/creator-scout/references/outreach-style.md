# Outreach style (optional drafts)

Drafts are optional and never sent by the tool; a person reviews and sends.

- Write in the creator's content language (`content_language`).
- Subject under 60 characters; body under 120 words; DM under 300 characters.
- Mention one specific recent video by what happens in it.
- Say in one sentence why their audience fits.
- Propose the suggested deal in plain words; do not quote a price.
- Friendly and direct. No hype, no emojis.
- Sign as "[Name], <brand>".
- End with one sentence saying we found their public profile and they can
  reply "no" to never hear from us again (first-contact transparency).
